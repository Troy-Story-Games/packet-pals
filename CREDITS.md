## Made for this project

- **Pip the Packet**, the flying packet critters, and the hub, switch and router pictures are drawn procedurally in code (`src/components/pip/`, `src/components/devices/`).
- **Background music** is generated live in the browser (`src/services/audio/music.ts`): random notes from a pentatonic scale over a looping chord progression. No recordings.
- **App icons** are generated by `scripts/generate-icons.mjs`.

No AI-generated art, audio or music is used in this project.
